Frequently asked

The practical
details.

Clear answers, without giving away the story.

Is this a subscription?

No. The entire 24-letter experience is purchased once. There is no recurring charge.

Does the recipient need an account or app?

No. The recipient does not need an email address, login, app, QR code, or any online action. They only need to open and read the mail.

When will the letters arrive?

A Cabinet postcard is mailed first. Daniel’s first letter is scheduled about seven days later, followed by one letter per week. Mail arrival dates are estimates and are not guaranteed.

Where can you send The Mud Letters?

Launch delivery is limited to valid United States mailing addresses.

Can I change the address later?

Yes. The buyer can update the address for future unsent mailings. Past mailing history remains unchanged.

What if a letter goes missing?

The buyer can report a missing letter and request a replacement for that specific letter through Manage Gift or support.

How do I reveal the gift?

Choose an in-person printable reveal, let the mailed Cabinet postcard reveal it, or start as soon as possible. The printable announcement is available immediately after successful payment.
